# Visual-target checklist

Use this when capturing real gameplay for `docs/VISUAL_REVIEW.md`. Capture at **1672 × 941** (or any 16:9) with the same camera and state as each target. Hex values are **guesses** sampled or eyeballed from the PNGs; tune them by eye in the real scene. Generated Hebrew and numbers are not authoritative.

## Shared look (all screens)

**Frame**
- The whole 3D viewport sits inside a **postcard**: a cream deckled paper border about 1.2 % of the width (`#F2DCBC`, darker torn-edge shadow `#C9A97E`), a soft inner shadow, and a postage stamp in the top-right corner with a wavy postmark.
- The stamp must be **generic** (an ibex silhouette, "יעלי", or a postmark). Never the place name.

**HUD**
- **Type:** Rubik, bold/heavy for titles and buttons, regular for body. Ink brown `#3B2A1E` on cream, cream `#F6ECD6` on green.
- **Top-left:** two round buttons, about 44 px, dark ranger-green `#2F4A23` with a 2 px light-cream ring, white icons (pause ❚❚, speaker / muted speaker). A third ⚙ appears only on the album screen.
- **Top-right:** a rounded case pill, dark green `#2E4228`, 2 px gold rim `#D4A64A` with an inner cream hairline. Title in cream heavy Rubik. Stars are gold `#F6C343`, empty star grey `#9AA08F`. The pill sits under the stamp.
- **Top-centre:** a sun-arc meter. A thin arc from a small sunrise icon (left) to a sunset icon (right), with the travelled part gold and the rest pale. The sun dot moves along it. A small cream label "שעות אור" sits under it.
- **Bottom-centre:** the tool belt, five round cream buttons (`#F3E3C3`, bevel shadow `#C9B48E`), about 82 px, each with a big colourful icon and a small Hebrew label under it.
  - Right to left: פטיש גאולוגי (hammer), מדריך שדה (green book with a flower), מד גובה (watch altimeter), מד גשם (blue cylinder), מצלמת עפיפון (orange kite + camera).
  - Under each, three charge pips: filled green `#5C8F2E`, used = hollow cream `#E9DDBF`.
  - Active tool: gold glow halo `#FBB46A` with a sunburst tick marks above it.
- **Right side:** the ranger notebook, a cream spiral-bound page (`#EFDDBF`, rings on top, a green elastic band `#4E6B2E` down the right edge) about 18 % of the width and 60 % of the height.
  - Header "יומן תצפיות של חוקר נוף" with a round tree logo.
  - Entries are handwritten-style bullet lines with a small grey sketch icon to the left. The newest entry has a yellow highlighter bar `#FDD878` behind it.
- **Bottom-left:** the big "למפה" button, dark green `#30411F`, gold-cream stitched border, folded-map + pin icon, » chevrons. It is the largest HUD button.
- **Hierarchy (largest to smallest):** למפה ≈ tool belt > notebook > case pill > round buttons > sun-arc.

**Light**
- Warm, low-sun painterly light. Long soft shadows and an atmospheric haze that lightens and cools with distance (Firewatch-like layered depth).
- Use no hard black shadows; shadow colour leans violet-brown.

**Characters**
- The ranger is chunky and readable: green cap and khaki shirt are the key silhouette, plus the backpack bump.
- יעלי reads at any size by the huge backward-curving horns.
